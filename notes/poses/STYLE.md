# Cast style (target look for every new character and every redraw)

Reference image: refs/cast_style.png (Scarlett, Nurse, Satsuki, Waves - the newer cast).
Attach it to every design prompt as "STYLE REFERENCE ONLY".

Style line to paste into prompts:
  "Match the art style of the style reference exactly: clean anime game-sprite art, thin dark-brown outlines, soft
  cel shading with gentle highlights, warm skin tones, expressive sharp eyes and a confident look. Chibi-leaning
  proportions like the reference: head about 1/4 of her height, very muscular build with big rounded shoulders and
  arms, thick defined thighs and calves, visible abs, wide fighting stance."

Notes:
- Everyone in the cast is heavily muscled; slim builds read as from another game (Sunny's old sprite).
- Old pixel-crunch look (thick black outlines, flat shading, giant head) is retired (old Goldie, twin-tails, kunoichi).
- Sizes: every fighter's stance frame packs to ~158-172 px tall in game; big builds (sumo, Vex) may go taller on purpose.

# Named poses (short names the user can use in requests)

Each entry: the name, what it is, the reference cut-out, and the prompt line to paste for a new character.
When the user says "give <character> a <name>", use the prompt line below with that character's description and the
character's own template, and attach the reference image as a POSE REFERENCE ONLY (copy the pose, not the character).

## TORPEDO DIVE
- What: airborne dive, body horizontal, falling straight down, head and chest facing the floor, one arm bent with the
  point of the ELBOW aimed straight down, that fist by her own chest, other arm tucked, legs together trailing straight
  out behind. Used as the Nurse's diving elbow drop (hits enemies lying on the floor).
- Reference: refs/poses/torpedo_dive.png (Nurse, jump fix sheet box 2)
- Prompt line:
  "TORPEDO DIVE: in the air, body horizontal and falling straight down, head and chest facing down toward the floor,
  one arm bent with the point of the ELBOW aimed straight down at the floor below her, that hand's fist by her own
  chest; her other arm tucked against her side; legs together trailing straight out behind her. Fierce, teeth gritted.
  Draw her high in the box, not touching the floor line."
- Pair it with the landing: "TORPEDO IMPACT: kneeling low, one knee down, leaning forward with her bent elbow and
  forearm driven flat into the floor in front of her knees; her other hand braced on her raised knee. Fierce shout."

## REAR JUMP KICK
- What: jump kick seen from behind (rear three-quarter): back of the shirt and head toward the camera, only the edge of
  the cheek visible, one leg kicking out at head height, the other tucked, arms out for balance.
- Reference: refs/poses/rear_jump_kick.png (Nurse)
- Prompt line (ChatGPT refuses a flat back view; this wording passes):
  "REAR JUMP KICK, rear three-quarter view: in the air, turned away from the viewer so we see mostly the back of her
  [loose top] and the back of her head, only the edge of her cheek visible. One leg kicking out to the right at head
  height, the other leg tucked under her, arms out to the sides for balance. Fully clothed, same outfit as box 1."
